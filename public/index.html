Site Layout Survey & Information Extraction Report

Physical Layout Summary

  • Total Cameras: 70 individually numbered IP cameras.
  • Support Poles: 32 poles (P1 through P32) along peripheral road and inner building perimeter.
  • Network Distribution Racks: 12 visible racks (8 Outer Ring OR1–OR8, 4 Inner Ring IR9, IR10, IR12, IR13).
  • Key Gates: Rear Gate (Upper-Left perimeter) and Main Gate (Bottom-Right perimeter).
  • Pickup & Drop Point: Located inside the building courtyard serving cameras 4 and 5.

Handwritten Legend & Color Coding

  • Green (Working - 41 Cameras): Cameras 13–16, 19, 35, 36, 37–70 (operating normally).
  • Red (Cable Issue - 15 Cameras): Cameras 1–6, 11, 12, 18, 21, 22, 26, 27, 28, 34.
  • Blue (Defective - 14 Cameras): Cameras 7–10, 17, 20, 23–25, 29–33.

Cabling & Network Architecture Notes

The site features a dual-tier ring topology:

  • Outer Ring (OR): Serves cameras mounted on perimeter poles (P13 to P27, P6 to P12). Each outdoor rack (OR1 to OR8) terminates outdoor-rated UV-resistant shielded Cat6 cabling from nearby poles and connects back to the server room via fiber optic backbone.
  • Inner Ring (IR): Serves cameras mounted around the building facade, inner perimeter poles (P1 to P5, P28 to P32), and courtyard areas. Racks IR9, IR10, IR12, and IR13 aggregate local PoE switches.
  • Cable Fault Diagnostics: 15 cameras suffer from physical cable termination issues, conductor degradation in conduit, or moisture ingress in junction boxes. These require tone tracing and re-crimping before hardware replacement.
